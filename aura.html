ABOUT THE PRODUCT

Redefining the Future of Human Vision.

Product Background

AURA VISION was developed as a next-generation augmented reality product concept designed to go beyond conventional smart glasses. While first-generation AR glasses focus on voice commands, gesture navigation, translation overlays, and AI-powered information, AURA VISION 3.0 explores more advanced ways for humans to interact with technology.

Purpose

The purpose of AURA VISION is to reduce dependence on smartphones, improve accessibility, enhance workplace safety, overcome language barriers, and provide intelligent real-time information through wearable technology.

Product Description

AURA VISION AR Glasses 3.0 combines advanced visual intelligence, neural interaction concepts, adaptive lenses, and spatial audio. Its companion smart contact lens concept explores a future where the physical glasses frame may be replaced by an ultra-compact visual interface.

  • Brain-computer interface interaction concept
  • AI-powered thermal and night vision assistance
  • Dynamic smart-tint technology
  • Spatial audio and bone-conduction concept
  • Advanced energy-harvesting research

NEXT-GENERATION INNOVATION

Advanced AR 3.0 Features

Six proposed upgrades designed to move beyond traditional augmented reality technology.

🧠

1. Brain-Computer Interface (BCI)

Neural sensors positioned around the glasses' temples and behind the ears explore the possibility of interpreting selected brain signals. Users could potentially control applications, dismiss notifications, and navigate interfaces without speaking or using hand gestures.

🧬

2. Bio-Synthetic Holographic Contact Lens

A futuristic lens concept designed to explore retinal projection, compact holographic displays, and bio-compatible materials. Research may investigate tear-powered biofuel cells as a potential supplementary energy source.

👁️

3. Adaptive Thermal & Night Vision

Advanced infrared sensors and AI image processing could help identify heat patterns, overheating equipment, and objects in low-light environments.

🕶️

4. Dynamic Smart-Tint Technology

Electronically adjustable lenses designed to control brightness and opacity based on the environment and user preferences.

🔊

5. Bone-Conduction Spatial Audio

Integrated bone-conduction audio and directional sound processing could improve voice clarity while reducing unwanted environmental noise.

🔋

6. Kinetic & Ambient Energy Harvesting

Future energy systems may combine transparent solar materials with kinetic energy harvesting. These technologies could supplement battery power and reduce charging frequency.

REAL-WORLD APPLICATIONS

Problems We Aim to Solve

01

Language and Cultural Barriers

Problem:

Travelers, professionals, and migrant workers may experience communication difficulties when interacting with people who speak different languages.


AURA VISION Solution:

Real-time AI translation could display translated subtitles in the user's field of view while spatial audio provides translated speech.

02

Commuter and Driver Safety

Problem:

Smartphone distractions, poor visibility, and delayed awareness of road hazards can increase transportation risks.


AURA VISION Solution:

Contextual navigation, object detection, and thermal assistance could provide additional hazard information without requiring users to look down at a phone.

03

High-Risk Industrial Repairs

Problem:

Technicians may struggle to identify equipment failures, overheating components, and technical repair procedures.


AURA VISION Solution:

AI visual recognition and thermal mapping could highlight potential problem areas and display step-by-step maintenance instructions.

04

Digital Fatigue and Text Neck

Problem:

Constantly looking down at smartphones can contribute to poor posture, neck strain, and reduced awareness of surroundings.


AURA VISION Solution:

Eye-level information and hands-free interaction could reduce unnecessary smartphone use.

05

Accessibility for Persons with Disabilities

Problem:

People with visual or hearing impairments may experience difficulties navigating public environments and communicating.


AURA VISION Solution:

AI-powered environmental descriptions, spoken alerts, live captions, and visual communication assistance could provide additional accessibility support.

06

Emergency Response and Public Safety

Problem:

Emergency personnel may face limited visibility, complex surroundings, and time-sensitive decision-making.


AURA VISION Solution:

Thermal imaging assistance, contextual information, and hands-free communication could support situational awareness.
